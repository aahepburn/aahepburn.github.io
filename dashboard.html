Financial Analytics

Executive Summary

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of financial performance across multiple dimensions. The dataset spans Sep 2013 to Dec 2014 and includes 700 transactions across 5 business segments and 5 geographic regions. The analysis aggregates over $118.7M in revenue with a 14.9% profit margin, enabling identification of high-performing segments, geographic opportunities, and product-level margin optimization targets.

Key Insights

  1. Government segment leads revenue generation at $52.5M, representing the largest revenue opportunity and strategic focus area.

  2. Geographic concentration shows the best performance in United States of America, accounting for $25.0M in revenue and indicating potential expansion opportunity. Other markets demonstrate strong sales, too.

  3. Product margin ranges from Velo at lower profitability to Amarilla at 16.5%, suggesting targeted margin improvement initiatives.

  4. Overall profit margin of 14.9% indicates healthy operational efficiency, with potential upside through segment-specific optimization.
