ShadowClip

Redact sensitive content from screenshots before they reach AI tools, colleagues, or support tickets. All detection runs on-device — nothing leaves your Mac or iPhone.

What it does

ShadowClip sits in your menu bar and intercepts screenshots the moment you take them. Before you paste, share, or upload anything, it automatically detects and blacks out the sensitive regions — faces, names, email addresses, API keys, access tokens, credit card numbers, and other structured credentials.

There is no cloud step. No account. No subscription. Detection runs entirely on-device using Apple Vision for faces and text layout, the Natural Language framework for named entities, and hand-authored pattern matchers for credentials and structured data. ShadowClip has no network entitlement and cannot make outbound connections.

Face & Person Detection

Apple Vision locates faces and person regions in screenshots. Redaction boxes cover the detected area with configurable padding.

Named Entity Recognition

The Apple Natural Language framework identifies names, organisations, and locations in any text recognised in the image.

Credential Patterns

Hand-authored regex patterns catch API keys, access tokens, connection strings, credit card numbers, and IBANs before they slip out.

Manual Redaction

Draw your own redaction boxes for anything automatic detection missed. Drag to resize, click to delete.

Clipboard Integration

The redacted image copies to your clipboard instantly — paste it wherever you were going without an extra save step.

Batch Export

Process a folder of existing screenshots in one pass. Redacted copies are saved alongside the originals.

Privacy by architecture

ShadowClip is built around a single constraint: your screenshots never leave your device. This is enforced at the architecture level, not by policy. The app has no network entitlement in its sandbox, which means the operating system will reject any outbound connection attempt, regardless of what the code tries to do.

No account is required. No telemetry is collected. No crash reports are sent. If you want to report a bug, you do it manually through the support address below.

All processing — OCR, entity detection, face detection, pattern matching — runs locally using Apple's on-device frameworks. The only data written to disk is the redacted image you choose to export.

System requirements

ShadowClip is available on two platforms:

macOS

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Apple Silicon recommended for best performance. Intel Macs supported.

iOS

iOS 17 or later. Works with the system share sheet — redact any image from Photos, Safari, or any other app.

Frequently asked questions

Does ShadowClip send my screenshots anywhere?

No. The app has no network entitlement. It cannot make outbound connections. All processing happens locally on your device.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No account and no subscription. ShadowClip is a one-time purchase on the App Store.

What languages does text detection support?

ShadowClip uses Apple's Vision and Natural Language frameworks, which support the languages available in those system APIs. Coverage is best for Latin-script languages. Credential pattern matching is language-agnostic.

Can I undo a redaction?

Yes — until you copy or export. Redaction boxes are non-destructive overlays in the editor. The original screenshot file is never modified; the redacted version is a new image.

Does automatic detection catch everything?

No automatic system catches everything. ShadowClip includes a manual redaction mode so you can draw boxes over anything the automatic pass missed. Always review the output before sharing.

Is there a family or team licence?

App Store Family Sharing is supported. For team deployments via Apple Business Manager, contact us at the address below.
