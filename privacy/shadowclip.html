Privacy Policy — ShadowClip

Quiet Signals Lab  ·  Last updated: 29 June 2026

ShadowClip does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. The images you process never leave your device.

What the app does with your images

Images are loaded into memory, processed, and discarded. The only file written to disk is the redacted output you explicitly choose to save, and where you choose to save it. The source image is never modified and never written to any location by the app.

On iOS, if you tap "Save to Photos", the redacted image is added to your photo library using the standard iOS Photos API. The app requests your permission before doing this and only writes the output — it does not read your library.

Audit sidecar

If you enable the optional audit sidecar, a .shadowclip-audit.json file is written alongside the output. It records the categories of redactions applied (e.g. "email address", "API key"), their pixel coordinates, and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the output file. It never contains the redacted text itself. This file is written locally to the same location you chose for the output. It is not transmitted anywhere.

Settings and preferences

App preferences (redaction style, detection category settings) are stored in UserDefaults on your device. On macOS, these are shared between the main app, the Share Extension, and the Quick Action via an App Group container. Nothing in this store is transmitted off-device.

No network access

ShadowClip has no network.client and no network.server entitlements in its App Sandbox. This is an operating system constraint, not a configuration option. The app cannot make outbound network connections. You can verify this with:
codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/ShadowClip.app

On-device frameworks

All detection uses Apple's on-device frameworks: Vision, Natural Language, and NSDataDetector. No data is sent to Apple for processing — these frameworks run entirely on your CPU/Neural Engine. No third-party SDKs or analytics libraries are included in the app.

Purchases

ShadowClip is sold as a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store. All payment processing is handled by Apple. Quiet Signals Lab receives no financial data, no payment information, and no App Store receipt data beyond what Apple's standard royalty reporting provides (aggregate sales counts, no individual identifiers).

No accounts, no tracking

There is no sign-in, no user account, no device identifier collection, no crash reporting, and no analytics. If you encounter a bug, you report it manually by email.

Children

ShadowClip does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. There is nothing to collect.

GDPR (EU/EEA users)

Because the app processes no personal data on behalf of users — all processing occurs on-device and no data is transmitted to Quiet Signals Lab or any third party — there is no data processing relationship to which GDPR applies. Changes to this policy
